```python
scores = [82, 9, 47, 92, 38, 82, 58, 91]
dic = {}
for m in scores:
    k = m // 10
    if k < 5:
        k = 5
    if k in dic:
        # 若键 k 在字典 dic 中
        dic[k] += 1
    else:
        dic[k] = 1
print(dic[5])
```
